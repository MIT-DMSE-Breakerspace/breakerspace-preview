Instrument reservations are not training appointments. Complete the training process before reserving independent instrument time.

## Reservation Rules At A Glance

| Rule | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| The trained reservation holder must be present | Make the reservation under your own Kerberos account and remain in the lab for the entire reserved session. |
| A reservation is held for 10 minutes | If the instrument is unoccupied 10 minutes after the reservation begins, the reservation holder forfeits priority and the instrument becomes available on a first-come, first-served basis. |
| The next reservation starts on time | If another user has the instrument reserved immediately after you, hand it over when their reservation begins. Reserve additional time later rather than running into their session. |
| Cancel time you will not use | Cancel as soon as you know you cannot attend so another user can reserve the instrument. |
| Guests may observe, but may not operate instruments | Only trained Breakerspace users may operate or control an instrument. Guests who want hands-on access should complete the relevant training. |

## Bringing Guests

A trained user may bring people who are not Breakerspace lab users into the lab as guests. The trained user must remain in the lab whenever their guests are present and is responsible for:

* Ensuring guests follow Breakerspace safety and lab-use rules.
* Making sure guests observe without operating or controlling the instrument.
* Making sure samples, shared tools, cleanup, and shutdown are handled correctly.
* Reporting any damage, unsafe condition, spill, or instrument problem promptly.

Only trained Breakerspace users may operate instruments. Do not share credentials or log in so that an untrained person can use an instrument. Guests who want hands-on access should follow the [Breakerspace training pathway]({{ "/training.html" | relative_url }}) and complete training for the relevant instrument.

## Before You Book

* Confirm that you have completed in-person training for the specific instrument and reviewed [Safety And Lab Use]({{ "/safety.html" | relative_url }}).
* Check the relevant [instrument page]({{ "/instruments/" | relative_url }}) for sample limits, preparation requirements, and the operating guide.
* Book only non-hazardous samples. Ask staff before booking if your material is unusual, difficult to prepare, outside the examples covered in training, or uncertain in safety or instrument compatibility.
* Estimate the whole session, including setup, sample loading, data collection, file export, cleanup, and shutdown.
* As an initial norm, keep each reservation to one hour or less. Contact staff before booking a longer uninterrupted session when the method genuinely requires it.
* Know where you will save your data. See [Files And Data]({{ "/resources.html#files-and-data" | relative_url }}) if you need a refresher.

## How To Book In LibCal

1. Open the calendar for the instrument you need.
2. Select a start time, then choose the end time. LibCal displays availability in 15-minute blocks.
3. Select **Submit Times** and sign in through MIT Touchstone with your Kerberos account.
4. Review the instrument, date, and full time range before completing the booking.
5. Keep the LibCal confirmation until the session is finished; it contains the information needed to manage the reservation.

## Use The Reservation Well

* Arrive with your samples prepared according to the instrument guidance.
* Start and finish within the reserved time, including data export, cleanup, and shutdown.
* Use the quick operating reference on the relevant [instrument page]({{ "/instruments/" | relative_url }}) while you work.
* Remove your samples and personal materials, return shared items, and leave the work area ready for the next user.
* Stop and ask for help if the instrument behaves unexpectedly or the sample no longer appears safe to run.

## Late Arrival, Cancellation, And Handoffs

Your reservation protects the instrument for the first 10 minutes. If you arrive more than 10 minutes late, you may still use the instrument if it remains available, but you may not displace someone who began using it after your reservation was forfeited.

If your plans change, use the management or cancellation link provided by LibCal and release the time as soon as you can. If you cannot find the booking information, email [dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu](mailto:dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu).

If your work is taking longer than expected and another reservation follows yours, save your work, complete the required shutdown or handoff steps, and turn over the instrument when the next reservation begins. Make a new reservation for the remaining work.

## Problems At The Time Of Your Booking

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Your tap access or workstation login does not work | Follow the steps under [Getting Access]({{ "/resources.html#getting-access" | relative_url }}). |
| The instrument is unavailable, damaged, or behaving unexpectedly | Stop. Find a Breakerspace staff member in the lab, ask in `#breakerspace-help` on [Slack](https://mit-dmse-breakerspace.slack.com), or email [dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu](mailto:dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu). |
| You need help choosing settings or interpreting results | Use the instrument page first, then follow the [advanced project help pathway]({{ "/resources.html#getting-help-with-advanced-work" | relative_url }}). |
| You need staff time rather than instrument time | Contact the team directly. Staff consultations are not currently booked through the instrument calendar. |
