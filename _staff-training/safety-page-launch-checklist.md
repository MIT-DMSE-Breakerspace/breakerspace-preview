# Safety Page Launch Checklist

This unlisted checklist tracks local details that should be confirmed before the public Safety and Lab Use page is considered complete.

## Confirmed In The Public Draft

* Emergency contact: MIT Police, Fire, and Ambulance at 617-253-1212, or 100 from a campus phone.
* Lab location for responders: Building 8, Room 8-102A.
* Instrument operation requires the relevant Breakerspace training.
* Guests may observe under a trained host's supervision but may not operate instruments.
* Breakerspace work is limited to non-hazardous materials unless a specific material and procedure receive advance staff approval.
* Gloves are used for sample preparation and loading or unloading, then removed before computer and workstation use.
* Food and drinks are allowed in the lounge and prohibited in the lab.
* Safety glasses are required for Instron and hardness-tester operation and for tasks that can produce particles, chips, fragments, dust, or splashes.
* There is no general closed-toe shoe requirement.
* Emergency and first-aid supplies are in the labeled cabinet above the sink.
* The emergency shower and eyewash are immediately to the left of the sink; the spill kit is in the same area.
* Routine trained use currently has no additional working-alone restriction, subject to the MIT Working Alone Policy and task-specific risk review.

## Local Decisions To Confirm

* Exact locations of the nearest fire extinguisher, fire alarm pull, and emergency exit route.
* Whether any additional incident or injury reporting form is required after immediate response.
* Whether an Instron-specific risk assessment identifies any operation that should not be performed alone.
* Whether the heavy Instron tooling assessment calls for protective footwear or additional handling aids beyond the current two-person and controlled-transfer guidance.

## Content And Signage

* Add clear photos or a simple location diagram for emergency and waste-disposal equipment after all locations are confirmed.
* Add photos showing the sharps container and ordinary trash and recycling bins.
* Confirm that posted signs in the lab use the same language as the website.
* Review the page with the DMSE Environment, Health, and Safety contact before launch if appropriate.
